What we are

Komputopedia is a small, personal initiative for learning and sharing computer science and AI. It started as an educational YouTube channel by three scholarship students and now continues as a one-person, AI-enabled studio. Ideas and judgement are human; AI helps with the execution.

KomputopedAI

Research and engineering, run as a one-person organisation supported by AI.

KomputoMedia

Short, simple explainers about computer science and AI for Indonesian students.

Komputopedia Kids

Playful learning for children, with its own channel and identity.

Our story

  1. The beginning

    Komputopedia was founded by three scholarship-funded students who wanted to give something back by sharing knowledge, mainly through educational videos on YouTube.

  2. A pause

    Over time the work became an obligation and lost its joy, so the channel went quiet.

  3. 2026: a fresh start

    Komputopedia returns to its original motivation: curiosity, enjoyment and meaningful sharing. It now runs as a one-person, AI-enabled initiative with three areas: KomputopedAI, KomputoMedia and Komputopedia Kids.

Vision and mission

Vision

A place where curiosity about computers and AI turns into knowledge that anyone, from children to university students, can enjoy and understand.

Mission

  • Explore ideas in computer science and AI because we are curious.
  • Make computer science feel easy and within reach for Indonesian students.
  • Offer playful learning for children through Komputopedia Kids.
  • Share openly and carefully: accurate, attributed and honest about the use of AI.
  • Keep it sustainable: curiosity before obligation, family and well-being first.
