# Kids Quest glossary

**Kids Quest**: the product. A family app where children earn points for good habits, receive pay, and manage savings.
**Asha Quest**: the Kids Quest instance for Asha.

**Family (Keluarga)**: the paying unit. Owns members, children, rule sets and all data. Families never see each other's data.
**Parent (Orang tua)**: a family member with their own login who approves, records, pays and edits rules.
**Child (Anak)**: a family member who logs in with a username and PIN, records activities and proposes new ones. A child can never approve.
**Platform admin**: Komputopedia staff who create families and curate public templates.

**Point (Poin)**: the unit a child earns or loses. Converted to money at payday at the family's **point value** (e.g. €0.10 per point).
**Activity (Kegiatan)**: something a child can do that changes points. Belongs to a **category** (Ibadah, Rumah, Akademis, …). Has one of three kinds:
- **Simple (Biasa)**: points × quantity.
- **Outcome (Pilihan hasil)**: a fixed set of outcomes, each with its own points (Sholat: Sendiri 0, Jamaah +1, Tidak sholat −1).
- **Weekly obligation (Wajib mingguan)**: a weekly quota with no points for meeting it, a **penalty** for missing it, and a **bonus** per unit above it.
**Quantity (Jumlah)**: how many units an entry covers (pages, verses).
**Entry (Catatan)**: one record of an activity on a date, with status pending, approved or rejected.
**Auto entry (Catatan otomatis)**: an entry the system derives rather than someone records: a missed daily outcome slot (unrecorded sholat) or a missed weekly obligation.
**Excused day (Libur)**: a day on which outcome-slot penalties do not apply (e.g. haid). Enabled per child by a parent.
**Proposal (Usulan)**: a child's request for a new activity and the points they ask for. A parent approves it once or adds it as a permanent activity, possibly with different points.
**Rule set (Aturan)**: a child's activities, categories and values. **Template**: a saved rule set a family can copy. Private by default; platform admins may promote it to a public template.

**Week (Minggu)**: Monday to Sunday.
**Period (Periode)**: two consecutive weeks. **Payday (Gajian)**: the Monday after a period, when a parent pays the period's points into savings.
**Savings (Tabungan)**: the child's money balance. It can go negative; a negative balance is **debt (Utang)** that later income pays off.
**Spending (Pengeluaran)**: money leaving savings, with a category set by parents.
**Charity (Sedekah)**: a spending category that also earns points (default: half the money's point value back as points).
