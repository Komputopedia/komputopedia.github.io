# Roadmap

## Now: Asha Quest v0.x (trial run, AO Family)

- Live at `/ashaquest/`, one family (AO Family), children Asha and Ayman.
- Goal of the trial: settle the rules, screen time and payday in daily use before a public release.

## Next: KidsQuest by KomputopediaKids (planned after December 2026)

- Public release under the name **KidsQuest by KomputopediaKids**.
- Open source on GitHub, in a `kidsquest` folder under Komputopedia.
- To prepare before release:
  - Rename the product and move the code from `ashaquest/` to `kidsquest/`, with Asha's setup as a template, not hard-coded.
  - English and Dutch interface text alongside Indonesian.
  - Self-service family sign-up and payment (subscription; free for friends at first).
  - Privacy policy and parental consent (GDPR: children's data, religious practice data).
  - Public template gallery curated by platform admins.
  - Weekly parent report with optional targets.
